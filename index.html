Privacy Policy for Telefono Targen

Last Updated: October 12, 2026 • Effective Date: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Telefono Targen (“we”, “our”, or “the App”), developed by Olti Gjomena, collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

  • Account Credentials: Name, phone number, email address, and encrypted (hashed) password. When using Apple or Google Sign-In, we receive authorized profile identifiers.
  • Vehicle Information: License plate numbers, vehicle brand/model, and photos of your registered vehicles.
  • Assigned Drivers: Contact information and names of secondary authorized drivers you assign.
  • Communications: Preset or custom alerts and photo evidence sent to notify vehicle owners.

B. Device & Technical Information

  • Device Identifiers: Operating system version, device model, and installation IDs for biometric binding and security.
  • Push Notification Tokens: Firebase Cloud Messaging tokens used exclusively to alert you of incoming driver messages.

2. Biometric and Facial Recognition Data Disclosures (Face ID)

Telefono Targen offers optional Face Authentication for swift and secure login. To protect your biometric privacy:

  • Processed Data: Facial scans are transformed into mathematical numerical vector embeddings. We do not store your raw facial photographs for authentication.
  • Strict Usage: Facial embeddings are used solely and exclusively to verify your identity upon login.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Biometric facial embeddings are never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers, data brokers, or external third parties.
  • User Control & Deletion: You can permanently remove your facial biometric data at any time via Profile > Face Recognition settings > Remove Face ID, or upon deleting your account.

3. Device Permissions

  • Camera (NSCameraUsageDescription): Used to scan your face for Face ID authentication and to capture live photos of parked vehicles.
  • Photo Library (NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription): Used to select vehicle pictures from your gallery or attach parking proof to messages.
  • Notifications: Used to deliver instant real-time alerts when someone contacts you about your vehicle.

4. 24-Hour Auto-Deletion of Communications

To preserve maximum privacy, all alerts, direct messages, and photo attachments exchanged between drivers automatically expire and are permanently purged from our active servers after 24 hours.

5. Data Security & Storage

All network communications between the mobile application and backend servers are secured using TLS/HTTPS encryption. We implement industry-standard safeguards to prevent unauthorized access.

6. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may edit your profile and registered vehicles at any time. Under Apple and global privacy guidelines, you may request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data directly within the app settings or by contacting our support team.
