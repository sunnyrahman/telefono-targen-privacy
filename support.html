Frequently Asked Questions

How do I contact a vehicle owner?

Simply enter the vehicle license plate number in the search bar. If the vehicle is registered, you can choose a quick preset message or write a custom note and attach a photo.

Are my messages private?

Yes. All conversations, alerts, and photo attachments automatically expire and are permanently purged from our servers after 24 hours.

How does Biometric Face ID work?

Face ID allows password-free sign-in. Your facial scan is transformed into a mathematical numerical embedding. We do not store your raw photographs. You can enable or remove it anytime in your Profile settings.

How can I request account or data deletion?

You can request account deletion directly from the Profile settings in the app or by sending an email to our support team.
